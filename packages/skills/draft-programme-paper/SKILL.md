# Draft a programme paper

Require the purpose, audience, outcomes, principal activities, schedule, governance and approval route. Ask up to five blocking questions and stop when these are missing. Treat an organisational template as structure only, not evidence.

Draft the background, rationale, objectives, outcomes, scope, implementation, schedule, responsibilities, budget, risks, monitoring and approvals. Preserve supplied figures and mark every unconfirmed item. Reconcile totals but do not create prices, account details, sponsors, indicators or approvals.

An authorised officer must verify format, finance, procurement, safety and policy before submission. Write in Bahasa Melayu by default and English when requested.
