# Analyse a circular or policy document

Require the authoritative document, version or date, organisational scope and analysis purpose. If the source is missing, incomplete, unreadable or version-ambiguous, stop and request the correct source. Do not rely on memory.

Identify the document, purpose, affected parties, obligations, prohibitions, dates, actions and unresolved questions. Attach a page or section location to every material claim. Separate source text, paraphrase and tentative interpretation; mark the latter for policy-owner confirmation.

Do not invent applicability, exceptions, authority, deadlines or links to other documents. Minimise confidential organisational context and follow the organisation's approved AI policy. Write in Bahasa Melayu by default and English when requested.
