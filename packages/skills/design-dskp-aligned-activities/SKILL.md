# Design DSKP-aligned activities

Create several feasible activity options for one teacher-confirmed Standard Kandungan or Standard Pembelajaran.

## Required source and inputs

Require:

- an official DSKP PDF or identifiable extract matching the subject and level;
- the exact target standard;
- available duration;
- general class context without identifying information;
- a closed list of materials and facilities; and
- known accessibility or safety needs.

Identify the document title, issuing authority, edition when available, standard code, exact wording and source location. Ask the teacher to confirm the standard before designing activities. If the source is absent, unreadable, conflicting or does not contain the target, stop and request the correct source. Do not use memory to fill a curriculum gap.

Ask no more than five concise questions at a time for missing blocking inputs. Do not request learner names, diagnoses or individual records.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the source and target standard.
2. Restate the observable learning focus without broadening the standard.
3. Treat time, materials and facilities as closed constraints.
4. Produce three genuinely different options: a core activity, a collaborative option and an alternative for a meaningful constraint.
5. For each option, specify timing, teacher action, learner action, participation, evidence of learning, optional support or extension, risks and teacher checks.
6. Compare the options for curriculum fit, feasibility, participation, evidence quality and safety.
7. Mark professional decisions `[PERLU SEMAKAN GURU]` or `[TEACHER REVIEW REQUIRED]`.
8. Finish with an unchecked teacher checklist.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent, merge, paraphrase as official, or silently broaden a DSKP standard.
- Do not add materials, apps, printing or devices outside the supplied list. Put useful extras in a separate unapproved-suggestions section and do not use them in the current activity.
- Treat implied materials as materials: writing, recording, worksheets, cards, picture guides, notebooks and whiteboard work are prohibited unless their required medium appears in the supplied list. State that the recording medium is unconfirmed rather than silently adding one.
- Do not propose concurrent stations when the confirmed quantity of equipment cannot support them.
- Do not label learners as weak, average or high. Describe flexible support and extension choices without assigning people to fixed groups.
- Do not diagnose or infer ability, motivation, family circumstances or disability.
- Do not reproduce substantial source text beyond the standard wording and brief context necessary for the task.
- Do not claim the activity is approved, safe or effective. The teacher decides after review.

Write in Bahasa Melayu by default and in English when requested. Preserve official Malay wording when an English explanation is also supplied.
