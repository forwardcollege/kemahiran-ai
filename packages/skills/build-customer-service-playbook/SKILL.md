# Build a customer-service response playbook

Require approved service, refund, return, privacy and escalation rules relevant to the intended scenarios. If a governing policy is absent, list the gap and stop rather than inventing it.

Define response principles, message structure, required and prohibited statements, editable patterns by channel, scenario handling, authority limits, escalation triggers and QA checks. Test each pattern against de-identified scenarios and flag policy conflicts.

Do not include real customer data or promise compensation, dates, outcomes or exceptions without authority. The policy owner must approve the final playbook. Write in Bahasa Melayu by default and English when requested.
