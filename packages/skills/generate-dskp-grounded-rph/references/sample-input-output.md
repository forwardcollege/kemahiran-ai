# Synthetic workflow example

This example shows expected behaviour, not a publication-ready RPH.

## Input

- Subject and level: Sains Demonstrasi, Tahun Contoh
- Topic: Sistem harian mudah
- Duration: 40 minutes
- Learner context: 25 learners; mixed confidence in oral explanation
- Resources: Teacher-supplied diagram and sticky notes
- Source: `example-dskp-extract.md`, explicitly selected for demonstration

## Expected source confirmation

Before drafting, the Skill should present:

- `DEMO-SK 1.0` — Pemerhatian sistem harian
- `DEMO-SP 1.1` — Mengenal pasti komponen
- `DEMO-SP 1.2` — Menerangkan hubungan
- Source: `example-dskp-extract.md`, “Standard demonstrasi” section
- Warning: synthetic demonstration only; not valid for a real RPH

It should then ask the teacher to confirm which demonstrator standard to use.

## Expected output characteristics

- The selected demonstrator standard is reproduced accurately.
- Activities and formative assessment align with the selected standard.
- Timing totals 40 minutes.
- Treat the teacher-supplied diagram and sticky notes as a closed allowlist. Cards, worksheets, notebooks and other common items are not added silently; any desired addition is proposed separately.
- Differentiation is expressed as optional scaffolds and extensions without labelling learners as high, medium or weak.
- Suggestions are marked separately from source-grounded requirements.
- No real student information appears.
- The final verification checklist remains unchecked for the teacher.
